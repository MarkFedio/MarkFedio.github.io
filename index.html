Bridging Profession & Passion

Why Physical Engineering Belongs on a Software Site

I'm a firm believer that computer science is engineering first and foremost. Engineers are tasked with using their hard and soft skills to solve problems. The medium in which us software engineers approach those problems (code) does not change that. When I build the projects presented below, I approach them in the same way I approach a software project: I identify a problem/task, I create a plan, and I relentlessly work toward a final product I'm proud of. My physical projects have honed my problem-solving, creative-thinking, prototyping, and debugging, as well as my discipline, ability to pivot, and knack for learning from failure. These are not computer science skills; they are engineering skills.
